Meet the Founders

We are two lifelong friends whose journey began in the classroom over 28 years ago. From being school and district toppers to excelling in competitive engineering entrance examinations, mathematics has always been at the heart of our academic journey.

After graduating from one of India's premier engineering institutions, we built successful careers in technology and consulting with leading global organizations, including Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle and PwC. Yet, throughout those years, one passion remained constant — teaching and mentoring students.

We believe that every student can excel in mathematics with the right guidance, strong fundamentals, and the confidence to think independently. That belief is what inspired us to start Math Minded.

Our goal is not just to help students score better marks, but to develop logical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a genuine appreciation for mathematics — skills that will benefit them far beyond the classroom.

For us, Math Minded is more than a coaching center. It is a commitment to nurturing young minds and helping every student discover their true potential.
